1. Share your location

A photo is only accepted together with the GPS position of this device, so that people looking at the same area can see where the traffic is. The position is saved with the photo; your name or account is never saved, and you cannot type a position in by hand.

2. Add a photo of the traffic

Take the photo where you are now. The picture is shrunk on your device before it is sent, and the server removes everything the camera wrote into the file.
